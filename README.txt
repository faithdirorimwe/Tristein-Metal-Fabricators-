Tristein Metal Fabricators - Static Website
===========================================

Files:
  index.html     Home
  about.html     About Us
  services.html  Services
  products.html  Products & Prices
  contact.html   Contact Us
  styles.css     All styling (responsive)
  script.js      Mobile menu, product filter, contact form
  images/        Photos, logo and favicon
  tristein-metal-fabricators-brochure.pdf

How to view:
  Double-click index.html to open it in any web browser.

How to publish:
  Upload the whole folder (keeping the structure) to your web host's
  public folder. index.html becomes the home page.

Note:
  The contact form only shows a thank-you message. To receive emails,
  connect it to a form service or your host's mail script.
