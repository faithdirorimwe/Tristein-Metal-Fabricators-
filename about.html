About us

Steel people, start to finish

Tristein Metal Fabricators (Private) Ltd is a Zimbabwean metal fabrication business operating from Msasa Park, Harare. We cut, weld, coat and install our own work, so the team that quotes your job is the team that finishes it.

Our story

What started as a small welding bay grew into a full fabrication workshop equipped for laser and plasma cutting, heavy structural work and precision aluminium installations. Over the years we have delivered pergolas and balustrades for homes, tank stands and palisade fencing for farms, and warehouse shades and steel roofing for industry.

Every job runs through the same discipline: accurate site measurement, honest material specification, clean welds, proper surface preparation and a finish that survives the weather.

  • Site measurement and drawings before fabrication
  • In-house cutting, welding, coating and installation
  • Workmanship guarantee on structures and installations
